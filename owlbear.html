Owlbear Rodeo

Share a room link, then put tokens on one scene

Owlbear is the battle map. The GM owns the room. Players open a URL, ask to join, and wait to be admitted. They do not need an Owlbear account unless they want to upload their own token image.

Official product: owlbear.rodeo. Short GM walkthrough: Getting Started. How rooms and invites work: Rooms. There is also a first-session video if someone wants to see the UI before call night.

GM: room, scene, invite

Create a free GM account and a room from the profile page (Creating a Room). Add one simple scene so you are not building a dungeon on the call. Copy the room URL from the address bar, or use Invite Players, and paste that link in Discord. When a join request pops up, admit the person. After AMBA export and WG join, place PC tokens and monster tokens on that scene. If export does not drop tokens for you, drag starter characters from the dock so the table still has something to move.

Example

GM creates room “AMBA workflow”, adds the basic Road map from a starter set, and posts https://owlbear.rodeo/room/… in Discord. Four join requests appear; GM admits each. They drop four PC tokens and two goblins, label one goblin “Goblin 1”, and ask Merry-Anchor to move only their token. If a player can delete the map, that is a permissions finding, not a reason to restart the room.

Player: link, waiting room, no account required

Open the GM’s room link in a browser. Set a display name (your test handle is fine). Ask to join and wait. Owlbear allows anonymous players: you can use tools that do not need saved assets. Create an account only if you must upload a custom token. Once admitted, move only what the GM said is yours. Keep WG on another window if you are checking stat blocks. You do not need AMBA as a player.

Example

Player opens the Discord link, types “Merry-Anchor”, clicks ask to join, and waits until the scene appears. They drag their scout token one square and stop. They do not need Google/Apple login for that. If they brought a PNG of the scout, they sign in first so the image can live on their account, then ask the GM how to add it to the scene.

What this test is actually checking

Does the invite link work for everyone? Can the GM admit people without a room password? Do PC and monster tokens exist after export, or only after hand placement? Can players move their tokens without editing the GM’s map? Stat blocks may stay in WG beside the scene. If the two tools disagree, write both observations on feedback.
