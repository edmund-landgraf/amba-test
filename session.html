Guide

First Remote Session

The goal is to test the toolchain together, not to run a real adventure yet.

Community and access

This first round is intended for trusted communities. The site does not store AMBA, WG, Owlbear, or Discord credentials. Signups use anonymous handles in the shared session view, and the only private signup detail to protect is the user's email address.

1

Gather in Discord

Everyone joins voice/chat. The GM starts screen sharing the AMBA GM view so the group can watch what appears from the campaign side.

2

Create or select characters

Players use their own accounts to create or choose one test character. The GM does not collect API keys, passwords, or account access.

3

Join the AMBA campaign

The GM shares the AMBA join key. Players join the campaign with their character, and the group confirms what becomes visible to the GM.

4

Pick a sample encounter

The group chooses a small set of monsters together, watches AMBA prepare/export encounter data, and notes anything confusing.

5

Export and test

Export to WG and Owlbear where supported. Run one short combat test and one DC challenge using WG UI.

6

Capture next steps

End by listing what worked, what broke, and whether this should become one longer session or two shorter sessions next time.
