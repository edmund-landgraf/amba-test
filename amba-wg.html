Guide

AMBA and WG Setup

Players control their own characters. The GM creates the campaign and shares the join key.

For the GM

  1. Create the AMBA campaign under your account.
  2. Open the GM view and start Discord screen share.
  3. Share the campaign join key with players.
  4. Watch which player characters appear after they join.
  5. Create a sample encounter and pick monsters with the group.
  6. Export to WG and Owlbear where the app supports it.

For players

  1. Sign in at AMBA Wanders Guide.
  2. Create or select one test character.
  3. Wait for the GM to share the campaign join key.
  4. Join the campaign with your character.
  5. Stay in Discord so the group can compare what each side sees.

Known uncertainty

WG player vs GM behavior

One purpose of the session is to learn exactly how WG behaves for players and GMs. The group will test visibility, permissions, combat controls, DC challenge flow, and exported stat block handling together.
