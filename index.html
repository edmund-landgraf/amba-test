What this is

Not a campaign session yet

This first session is about watching the tools work together. The GM will create an AMBA campaign, share the join key, screen share the GM view, and guide everyone through the test. We will learn what the GM can see after player characters join, then try the export path into WG and Owlbear.

Requirements

  • Discord for voice/chat
  • PC or Mac
  • Modern browser
  • AMBA/WG account for your character

What to bring

  • One test character, or time to create one
  • Your Discord handle
  • Availability for proposed session times
  • Questions about AMBA, WG, or Owlbear

What not to bring

  • No API keys
  • No account passwords
  • No campaign prep required
  • No actual play expectations yet
